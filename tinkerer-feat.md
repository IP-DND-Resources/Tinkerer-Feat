# Tinkerer (Feat)
Magic items are cool, but you know what's more cool? Controlling a battlefield using the same items that any normal person could use. Problem is, they aren't really mechanically viable with their lower success chance (DC). This feat improves the likelyhood of the item's effect succeeding by imposing Disadvantage which is effectively a -5 penalty their roll (or a +5 to the DC). Additionally, it gives the character a basic currency that can be used to consistently make items needed for combat or other situations to support the party.

## Tinkerer
*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Dexterity or Intelligence 13+)*

You have a knack for making and utilizing non-magical equipment. You gain the following benefits.
:
**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Dexterity or Intelligence score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
:
**Tinkerer.** Creatures of your choice have Disadvantage on Dexterity checks to avoid, escape, resist, or end effects of non-magical items you deploy.
:
**Salvager.** As part of a Short or Long Rest, you can search the area around you for scrap materials. Make a DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check. On a success, you find 3d4 pieces of scrap material. 

Each piece of scrap weighs 0.1 pounds, has no value, and can be used to replace the crafting cost of non-magical items by 1 SP each.
:
With 1 hour of light work or when you finish a Short or Long Rest you can perform one of the following:

**Scrapper.** You can scrap a non-magical item. For each pound the scrapped item weighs, you receive 1 piece of scrap material. 

**Crafter.** You can craft one non-magical item that uses a Tool you have Proficiency with, that isn't a weapon or armor. You must have the required amount of scrap for the required GP cost.

## PHB 5.5 Crafting Rules
Under regular crafting rules, you need to use a Tool that you have proficiency with that can craft the item, an amount of gold (for raw materials) equal to half of the item's value, and a number of crafting days (8 hours per day, minimum 1 day) equal to its regular GP value divided by 10. This is quite cumbersome as an item like Caltrops would cost 5 SP, but take an entire day to make.
:
See PHB 5.5 (2024) Page 233 for more details.

### Scrap Crafting
This feat enables you to periodically find "scrap materials" while you take a Short or Long Rest, or by breaking down existing items you have that you don't want. This scrap is worthless to any merchants because it is simply bits of metal, springs, fibers, oils, etc. 

Scrap material crafting enables you to efficiently and relatively quickly craft basic items you need while you are out adventuring

##### Example: Caltrops
Caltrops would normally take 5 SP and one day to craft with Smith's Tools. With the **Crafter** benefit, you can make Caltrops with **5 pieces of scrap** during your Short or Long Rest.

##### Example: Hunting Trap
Hunting Trap would normally take 2 GP, 5 SP, and one day to craft with Tinker's Tools. With the **Crafter** benefit, you can make Hunting Trap with **25 pieces of scrap** during your Short or Long Rest.

##### Example: Dynamite (Black Powder)
Dynamite would normally take 1 pound of Black Powder, 32 GP, 5 SP, and 1 Hour of focused work (not during a Short or Long rest) to craft with Tinker's Tools or Alchemist's Supplies. With the **Crafter** benefit, you can make Dynamite with 1 pound of Black Powder and **325 pieces of scrap** during your Short or Long Rest.

### DM Note - Buying Scrap
If your DM permits it, you may be able to purchase scrap from a merchant by offering to purchase broken or decayed items they no longer want for a mutually attractive price. The reccomended amount of scrap a merchant has is 3d4 pieces at a price of 1 SP each.
